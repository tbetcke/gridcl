## Scope summary

Hosted CI's `vulkan-11` job runs only `high_order_invariance::vector_size_invariance` at p = 3 and p = 4,
in one test process on Mesa lavapipe, under the job's `timeout-minutes: 40`. On main's run 44833612710
(5612386) the build took 6 min 30 s and the two tests 885 s (job 22 min); on the next run, 44833632046
(3cf9401, a plan-only commit), the build took 7 min 41 s, p = 3 finished after 30 min 25 s with p = 4 still
running, and the job was cancelled at 40 min. The other vulkan jobs took 9–24 min on those two runs. E3 moves p = 4 into a
shard 12 of `scripts/ci-tetraloma-shard.sh` with a vulkan job of its own, keeps the 40-min limit, and adds
a check that shards 1–12 still run every test of tetraloma exactly once. Maintenance of hosted CI only: no
library code, public API, crate boundary, convention or acceptance test changes.

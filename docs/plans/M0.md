## Scope summary

M0 creates the Cargo workspace of "Workspace layout" as skeleton crates on E0's Rust 1.99 pin,
with `cubecl = "=0.11.0"` in `[workspace.dependencies]`; gridcl-testing with `test_client()`,
which selects the device from `GRIDCL_TEST_DEVICE` locally and in `scripts/hpc` jobs; the
gridcl-mpi-tests skeleton with `scripts/mpi-test.sh`; and the hosted CI jobs cpu, mpi-mpich,
mpi-openmpi and vulkan plus skeletons of the conformance and ptscotch jobs. It also closes the
three E0 review follow-ups.
